POKEMON TACTICS - 56 RECRUITABLE FAMILIES AND THEIR EVOLUTIONS

Pokemon artwork:
The user states that the Pokemon assets are authorized for this project.
The POC reuses the same public artwork sources already referenced by this
repository's Pokemon Tower Defense game. These are NOT CC0 assets.
Names and moves identify Pokemon; the POC's stats, initiative, energy,
type multipliers and cooldowns are adaptations for tactical play, not the
official battle simulation.
The eight combat elements are simplified tactical archetypes, not claims
about canonical Pokemon typing or learnsets. New families reuse the existing
move vocabulary and spell icons; regional, Mega and Gigantamax forms are
excluded. Evolution requirements are simplified to tactical gold costs.
The 50 added standard-form evolution lines were checked against PokeAPI's
species and evolution-chain JSON in https://github.com/PokeAPI/api-data
(data/api/v2/pokemon-species/ and data/api/v2/evolution-chain/).
Perrserker is excluded because it evolves only from Galarian Meowth.
English and Spanish names are shared for these species; Hebrew names are
transliterations and Chinese names use Simplified Chinese.
